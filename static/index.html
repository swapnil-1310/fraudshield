Investor Awareness

Common Warning Signs of Financial Scams

Learn to recognize typical tactics used to deceive retail investors.

Guaranteed High Returns

Promises of fixed daily or monthly gains (e.g. "30% profit guaranteed") are the most reliable hallmark of investment fraud. Market investments always carry risk.

Artificial Urgency & Deadlines

Claims that "only 5 spots remain" or "offer expires in 10 minutes" are pressure tactics designed to force action before you can verify credentials.

Private Chat Groups

Invitations to join private VIP WhatsApp or Telegram channels for "exclusive insider tips" are typically operated by unregistered and unregulated entities.

Requests for Credentials or OTP

Legitimate investment advisers never ask for your Demat trading password, banking OTP, or UPI PIN. Never share authentication credentials.

Celebrity & Executive Deepfakes

Scammers use AI to clone voices and animate videos of well-known public figures and business leaders endorsing fake investment schemes.

Overview

How FinGuard Works

Understanding the inspection process and how to interpret your results.

What You Submit

You can upload any suspicious financial video clip, audio message, screenshot, message text, website URL, or Android APK. The platform validates file formats and ensures content stays within the 100 MB upload limit.

What the System Checks

FinGuard evaluates media authenticity and financial scam indicators as distinct, independent signals:

  • Video Authenticity: Extracts sampled frames and inspects detected face regions for synthetic manipulation.
  • Audio Authenticity: Evaluates 5-second speech segments for synthetic voice cloning and known persona impersonation.
  • Financial Intent & Compliance: Transcribes spoken speech and checks for guaranteed returns, artificial deadlines, and unregistered advisory claims.
  • SEBI Registry: Checks claimed names and registration numbers against the official SEBI intermediary database.
  • Security Forensics: Audits links for typosquatting and APKs for dangerous permissions like SMS interception.

What the Results Mean

Reports use transparent, calibrated consumer labels:

  • No clear warning signs found: The content scored below thresholds across all evaluated modalities.
  • Review recommended: Signals are ambiguous or credentials could not be verified; independent caution is advised.
  • Possible scam detected: The speech or text contains documented regulatory violations or high-risk scam solicitations.
  • Possible AI-generated or altered content: Visual or voice detectors identified synthetic deepfake artifacts.

What You Can Do Next

Every report includes actionable next steps: verifying credentials on the official SEBI website (sebi.gov.in), avoiding fund transfers, or downloading an automated complaint draft for submission to SEBI SCORES 2.0 (scores.sebi.gov.in).

Architecture

Pipeline Technology & Architecture

An honest, transparent view of the engineering pipeline without marketing exaggerations.

Multi-Modal Defense in Depth

FinGuard is designed around a multi-stage defense architecture. No single model makes the final decision. Visual signals, acoustic models, semantic language parsers, and official registry lookups operate as separate checkpoints. When a detector fails or is unavailable, the pipeline marks that modality as degraded or inconclusive rather than inventing a result.

Calibration & Regulatory Rules

The final verdict is calibrated against concrete regulatory standards. An AI-generated voice alone triggers an authenticity warning, but does not independently establish fraud if the spoken content is harmless education. Conversely, a genuine human voice promoting fraudulent Ponzi schemes is flagged as a scam even without deepfake manipulation.

System Limitations & Human Oversight

FinGuard is an assistive decision-support tool, not an infallible legal judge. Heavy compression, low video resolution, or background noise can reduce forensic certainty. Users are always encouraged to verify suspicious claims through official regulatory registries before making financial commitments.
